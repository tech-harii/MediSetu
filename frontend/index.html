How The System Protects Rural Patients

1
AI Intake & Triage in Telegram
Patients chat in English or Tamil. The bot asks gentle symptom questions, detects emergencies (directing them to 108 immediately), and never prescribes drugs.
2
Volunteer Doctor Video Consultation
Doctors see cases sorted by urgency (Red → Yellow → Green). One click launches an instant, zero-login private video room and notifies the patient on Telegram.
3
Verified Volunteer Pickup & OTP Handover
Volunteer buys medicine at a registered pharmacy, uploads the bill receipt with exact amount, and delivers to patient. Delivery is unlocked strictly via 6-digit OTP.

Zero-Bandwidth Emergency SOS SMS

In remote Western Ghats & Eastern Ghats sections (Valparai, Meghamalai, Jawadhu Hills) where 4G/5G mobile internet drops completely, life-or-death RED cases cannot wait for video calls. The system instantly falls back to GSM 900MHz SMS, beaming GPS coordinates to the 108 Ambulance fleet, notifying the nearest Primary Health Centre (PHC), and sending Tamil first-aid instructions.

🚑 108 Hill Fleet Auto-Dispatch 🏥 PHC Casualty Notification ⏱️ Golden-Hour Bystander SMS
📍 Sub-Meter GPS Coordinates
Dispatches exact Lat/Lng coordinates directly into the 108 ambulance driver's device for turn-by-turn navigation through ghat hairpin bends.
🏥 Nearest PHC Doctor Alert
SMS alerts the duty Medical Officer at the closest Primary Health Centre to prepare oxygen, IV cannula, and defibrillator before patient arrival.
🗣️ Tamil Golden-Hour Protocol
Sends immediate bystander instructions in Tamil preventing fatal mistakes (e.g. recovery position for stroke, avoiding oral fluids).
